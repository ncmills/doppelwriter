A resignation letter is one of the few pieces of writing where AI is genuinely a good fit: it's short, formulaic, and nobody wants it to be creative. The risk isn't that the letter will be bad. It's that the AI will quietly state something false, such as the wrong last day or a made-up reason for leaving, and you'll hit send without noticing. Here's how to use AI for this without those problems.

## Can you use AI to write a resignation letter?

**Yes. A resignation letter is a short, conventional document that AI can draft in seconds, as long as you supply the facts and review the output.** You aren't alone in considering it: in a TonerGiant survey, a quarter of UK workers said they would use AI to write a resignation letter ([TonerGiant via ResponseSource](https://pressreleases.responsesource.com/news/106299/one-in-three-uk-jobseekers-now-use-ai-to-write/)). That figure measures stated intent in the UK, not actual behavior, so treat it as a rough signal rather than proof of how common this is.

The job of the AI is wording. The job of the facts is yours.

## What should a resignation letter include?

**Contact information, your manager's name, a clear statement that you're resigning, your last day of employment, a short thank-you, and a sign-off.** That's the checklist Indeed gives for a professional resignation letter, with sign-offs like "Sincerely" or "Best regards" ([Indeed Career Advice](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/career-development/resignation-letter-example)).

One prompt guide recommends keeping it under 150 words across three paragraphs: resignation and last day in the first sentence, one specific line of thanks, and a handover offer ([Whizi](https://whizi.io/resources/chatgpt-resignation-letter-prompt/)). Short is a feature here. A resignation letter is a record, not a farewell speech.

## What does AI get wrong in a resignation letter?

**It can't know your notice period or exact last day, and a vague prompt can lead it to invent a reason for leaving.** Both are documented problems.

- **Made-up facts.** A prompt guide warns that your prompt should contain every fact the letter states, typed by you (title, manager, notice period, exact last day), because ChatGPT can't know them. Notice period is the one it gets wrong when it guesses ([Whizi](https://whizi.io/resources/chatgpt-resignation-letter-prompt/)).
- **Invented reasons.** Another guide says specifying "no mention of future employment" stops ChatGPT from inventing a reason on your behalf, which it will do if the prompt is vague ([Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/chatgpt-prompts-for-writing-a-resignation-letter/)).

<Callout>
Before sending, read the letter once purely for facts: name, title, date, last day. A fluent sentence with a wrong date is worse than a clunky sentence with the right one.
</Callout>

## What's a good prompt for an AI resignation letter?

**A good prompt gives the AI your facts, a length cap, a tone, and explicit things to leave out.** Here's a template:

```
Write a resignation letter from me to my manager.

Facts (use exactly these, add nothing else):
- My name: Jordan Lee
- My title: Senior Analyst
- Manager: Priya Raman
- Date of letter: December 1
- Last day of work: December 15

Requirements:
- Under 150 words, three short paragraphs
- First sentence states that I'm resigning and my last day
- One specific line of thanks (I'm grateful for the mentoring on the Q3 forecasting project)
- Offer to help hand over my work
- Warm but plain tone, no corporate buzzwords
- Do not give a reason for leaving and do not mention future employment
```

Every fact is yours, the structure follows the length guidance above, and the last line blocks the invented-reason problem. The specific thanks line is what keeps it from reading like a template: name a real project or person.

## Do I have to give two weeks' notice?

**No. In the US there is no legally mandated notice period for individual resignations; two weeks is a common convention that isn't legally binding.** ([CXC Global](https://www.cxcglobal.com/global-hiring-guide/united-states/end-of-employment-in-united-states/)). The exception is a contract or written policy tied to your employment terms, which can make a longer notice period binding ([HR Cloud](https://www.hrcloud.com/resources/glossary/notice-period)).

If you do give two weeks, that usually means 10 business days ([USLawExplained](https://uslawexplained.com/notice_period)). Count the actual calendar date yourself and type it into the prompt; don't let the AI do the arithmetic. This article covers US norms only, and rules differ elsewhere.

## Do I have to explain why I'm leaving?

**No. A resignation letter doesn't need a detailed justification; a general reason like a new opportunity, school, or personal reasons is enough.** ([Indeed Career Advice](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/career-development/resignation-letter-example)). You can also skip the reason entirely. A TSA directive, quoted in a federal-employee resignation guide, says an employee "may choose not to provide a reason for leaving and may so state on the notification" ([FedUp](https://fedup.work/guides/federal-employee-resignation-letter)).

Leaving the reason out is also the safest instruction to give an AI. It can't embellish what you told it not to write.

## Is it safe to put your details into ChatGPT?

**Keep it minimal. OpenAI says that for individual services like ChatGPT it may use your content to train its models, and you can opt out through its privacy portal or Data Controls** ([OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6783457-chatgpt-general-faq)). OpenAI also says users shouldn't share sensitive information they wouldn't want to be used or reviewed ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/how-chatgpt-protects-privacy/)).

A resignation letter rarely needs anything sensitive. You can leave out details like salary, disputes with colleagues, or confidential project specifics. Those sources only cover OpenAI's stated practices; other tools handle data differently, so check the privacy terms of whichever one you use.

## How do you make an AI resignation letter sound like you?

**Give the AI a sample of your real writing and edit out anything you wouldn't say out loud.** Default AI output tends to be generic and stiff, which is fine for a form letter but odd coming from someone your manager has worked with for years.

Two quick fixes:

1. **Paste a short email you wrote** and add "match this tone and sentence length."
2. **Read the draft aloud.** Cut or rewrite every line that makes you wince.

## FAQ

### Is it okay to use AI to write a resignation letter?

Yes. It's a short, formulaic document that suits AI drafting. Supply the facts yourself, proofread, and edit so it sounds like you.

### Do I have to give two weeks' notice?

Not by law, in the US. Two weeks is a convention unless a contract or written policy says otherwise, so check your agreement first.

### Do I have to explain why I'm leaving?

No. A general reason or none at all is enough.

### Is it safe to paste my details into ChatGPT?

OpenAI advises against sharing sensitive information and says it may train on individual-account content unless you opt out. Keep the letter's inputs minimal.

### Why does my AI resignation letter sound so stiff?

AI defaults to generic phrasing. Give it a tone instruction, a length limit, and a sample of your writing, then cut anything you wouldn't say aloud.
